About Texacoderzz

How a website project works

A project starts with a free, no-obligation scoping call, where we discuss what you need. Once you approve a scope and price, we confirm it in writing along with a payment schedule, typically an upfront deposit to begin work and a remaining balance due on delivery. Pricing shown on our Pricing page is a starting-price guide for indicative packages; the exact price for your project depends on its scope and is agreed before any work begins.

What's included

Each project includes a mobile-responsive build and an engineer review before launch. Reasonable revisions within the agreed scope are included; requests that go meaningfully beyond the original brief are scoped and quoted as additional work, agreed with you before we proceed. You agree to provide the content, images, and access we reasonably need (e.g. existing branding, copy, or accounts) in good time so we can keep to the agreed timeline.

Domain & hosting

Domain registration and hosting are billed separately from the design/development price, at cost, and are provided through third-party registrars and hosting providers. Those providers' own terms apply to the domain and hosting itself.

Payment & ownership

Once a project is paid in full, ownership of the delivered website and its assets transfers to you. See our Refund & Cancellation Policy for how deposits, cancellations, and the final balance are handled.

We use AI to draft the first pass of layout, structure, and copy for a project, and our engineers review, refine, and take responsibility for every build before it reaches you. You're contracting Texacoderzz for the finished, human-reviewed result, not for AI output on its own.
