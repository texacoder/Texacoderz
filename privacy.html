Privacy Policy

What we collect when you reach out about a project or use this site, and why.

Last updated: September 2026

Who we are

Texacoderzz is a student-led web development studio based in Pathanamthitta, Kerala, India, building websites for shops, startups, and small businesses. This policy covers the information we collect through our website, mainly through project enquiries.

What we collect

  • Project enquiries: the name, email address, and message you submit through our contact form when reaching out about a website project.
  • During a project: whatever content, branding, or account access you choose to share with us so we can build your site (for example, existing copy, images, or hosting logins). We use this only for your project.
  • Buy Us a Coffee: separately from client projects, our small "Buy Us a Coffee" feature may collect an email address, only if you choose to enter one for a payment receipt. It's optional.

We never see or store your card, UPI, or bank details for any payment made on this site. Client project payments are arranged directly with us (bank transfer, UPI, etc.), and Buy Us a Coffee payments are handled entirely by Razorpay, a licensed payment gateway; we only receive a payment confirmation and identifiers afterward.

How we use it

To respond to your project enquiry, provide a quote, deliver and support your website, and keep basic business and financial records. If you provided an email for a Buy Us a Coffee contribution, we use it only to send a payment confirmation. We don't sell, rent, or share your information for marketing, and we don't run advertising or tracking pixels on this site.

Third-party processors

We use Web3Forms to relay contact form submissions to our inbox, and Razorpay to process Buy Us a Coffee payments. Each operates under its own privacy policy and security standards.

Data retention & security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. We keep enquiry, project, and contribution records only as long as needed to deliver a project, respond to you, or maintain basic business records, and we take reasonable steps to keep what we hold secure.

Your rights

You can ask us to tell you what information we hold about you, or to delete it, at any time. Email texacoderzz@gmail.com and we'll action it promptly.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page with a new "last updated" date. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the update.

Contact

Questions about this policy: texacoderzz@gmail.com or WhatsApp.
