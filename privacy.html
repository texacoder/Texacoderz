Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and how it's handled. Kept short on purpose.

Last updated: September 2026

Who we are

Texacoderzz is a student-led web development studio based in Pathanamthitta, Kerala, India. This policy covers the information collected through texacoderzz's website, including the contact form and the "Buy Us a Coffee" support feature.

What we collect

  • Contact form: the name, email address, and message you submit when reaching out about a project.
  • Buy Us a Coffee: an email address, only if you choose to enter one for a payment receipt. It's optional. You can support us without providing it.
  • Payment details: we never see or store your card, UPI, or bank information. Payments are handled entirely by Razorpay, a licensed payment gateway; we only receive a payment confirmation and identifiers (order ID, payment ID) after your payment goes through.

How we use it

Solely to respond to your enquiry, send a payment confirmation if you provided an email, and keep basic records of contributions received. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing, and we don't run advertising or tracking pixels on this site.

Third-party processors

We use Razorpay to process payments and Web3Forms to relay contact form submissions to our inbox. Each operates under its own privacy policy and security standards; we encourage you to review Razorpay's policy if you have payment-specific privacy questions.

Data retention & security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. We keep contact and contribution records only as long as needed to respond to you or maintain basic financial records, and we take reasonable steps to keep what we do hold secure.

Your rights

You can ask us to tell you what information we hold about you, or to delete it, at any time. Email texacoderzz@gmail.com and we'll action it promptly.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page with a new "last updated" date. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the update.
