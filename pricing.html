Common questions

DOMAIN & HOSTING

Is a domain name included?

No — pricing above is for design and development only. A domain typically costs ₹500–₹1,500/year and hosting ₹0–₹3,000/year depending on what your site needs; we'll help you pick and set both up.

PAYMENT

When do I pay?

We'll confirm scope and price on a quick call, then agree on a payment schedule — usually half upfront, half on delivery — before work starts.

MAINTENANCE

What does the ₹499/month plan cover?

Content updates, small fixes, and uptime checks so your site keeps working without you having to touch code.

CUSTOM WORK

My project doesn't fit these tiers — now what?

See the custom pricing section above, or message us directly on the contact page — we'll scope it and quote a fixed price before anything starts.
