Refund & Cancellation Policy

How we handle payment issues, mistakes, and cancellations.

Last updated: September 2026

Buy Us a Coffee contributions

Buy Us a Coffee payments are voluntary, one-time contributions made to support Texacoderzz's work — not a purchase of goods or a service with a fixed deliverable. Because of this, contributions are generally non-refundable once processed.

When we will issue a refund

We will investigate and issue a refund if:

  • You were charged more than once for the same contribution (a duplicate transaction).
  • You were charged an incorrect amount due to a technical error on our end.
  • The payment was unauthorized or fraudulent, and you report it promptly.

To request a refund for one of these reasons, email texacoderzz@gmail.com within 7 days of the payment, including the payment ID or order ID from your Razorpay confirmation email/receipt. We'll review and respond within a few business days.

How refunds are processed

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method via Razorpay. Depending on your bank or payment method, funds typically reflect within 5–7 business days after we initiate the refund — this timeline is set by Razorpay and your bank, not by us.

Cancellations

Since Buy Us a Coffee is a one-time, immediate payment (not a subscription), there is nothing to "cancel" once a payment is confirmed. If your payment fails, is cancelled in the checkout window, or times out before completion, you have not been charged and no further action is needed.

Client website projects

Refunds, cancellations, and payment schedules for client website projects (see Pricing) are not processed through this website — they are agreed individually in writing with each client before work begins, and are governed by that agreement rather than this policy.
