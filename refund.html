Refund & Cancellation Policy

How deposits, cancellations, and refunds work for website projects, and everything else we bill for.

Last updated: September 2026

Scoping calls

Getting a quote is always free and non-binding. Nothing is charged, and no cancellation policy applies, until you approve a scope and price and we agree a payment schedule in writing.

Deposits & starting work

Once you approve a quote, we typically ask for an upfront deposit before starting, with the remaining balance due on delivery. If you cancel before we've started any work on your project, your deposit is refunded in full. Once work has started, the deposit covers the time already committed and is generally non-refundable, though we'll always be reasonable. If you cancel partway through, we'll work out fairly with you what's owed based on work actually completed.

Final balance & delivery

The remaining balance is due when the site is ready for delivery. The completed site, its files, and access credentials are handed over once the final balance is settled.

Revisions

Reasonable revisions within your project's agreed scope are included at no extra cost. Changes that go meaningfully beyond the original brief are treated as additional work and quoted separately before we proceed, so there are no surprise charges.

Domain & hosting

Domain and hosting are billed separately from the design/development price, at cost, through third-party registrars and hosting providers. Because we pay these providers directly on your behalf, these costs are non-refundable once purchased, the same as they would be if you bought them yourself.

Maintenance plans

If you're on an ongoing maintenance plan, you can cancel anytime for future billing periods. We don't refund the portion of a period that's already been paid for and is in progress.

Buy Us a Coffee

Separately from client project payments above, this site also has a small "Buy Us a Coffee" feature for voluntary, one-time support contributions. These are generally non-refundable, since nothing is being purchased or delivered in return. We will refund a contribution if you were charged twice for the same payment, charged the wrong amount due to a technical error, or the payment was unauthorized. To request one, email texacoderzz@gmail.com within 7 days with your payment ID from the Razorpay confirmation. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method and typically take 5–7 business days, a timeline set by Razorpay and your bank, not by us.

Contact

Questions about a project payment, deposit, or refund: texacoderzz@gmail.com or WhatsApp.
