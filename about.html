about

Student engineers. Real engineering standards.

Texacoderzz is a student-led web development startup, proudly supported by Musaliar College of Engineering, Pathanamthitta. We combine the speed of AI with human creativity and careful review, so every site we ship is fast to build and built to last.

story

Where we started

We started with a simple goal: help businesses, startups, and individuals build a strong online presence through modern, affordable, high-quality websites. AI handles the first draft — layout, structure, starting copy — and our team takes it from there, reviewing every build for performance, responsiveness, and reliability before it reaches a client.

We believe every business, big or small, deserves a professional website that builds trust and attracts customers. As a growing startup, we're committed to honest service, transparent communication, and continuous support as our clients grow.

process

How a project moves through the studio

01 · BRIEF

You tell us what you need

A quick call or message about your business, your pages, and what the site needs to do for you.

02 · DRAFT

AI generates a first pass

Layout, structure, and starting copy come together fast, giving us a real draft to react to instead of a blank page.

03 · ENGINEER REVIEW

Our team refines every detail

Spacing, responsiveness, copy, and performance get checked line by line before anything is called done.

04 · LAUNCH

Your site goes live

We hand over a working, responsive site — with an optional maintenance plan if you want us to keep looking after it.
